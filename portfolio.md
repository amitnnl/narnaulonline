## Project 03 — Torvo Multibrand Power Tools Commerce Platform

**Category:** B2B E-Commerce, Inventory, Accounting

**Overview:**

A proposed commerce platform for a multibrand power-tools and accessories business, designed to support a controlled ordering process between registered buyers and the seller.

**Core workflow:**

1. Buyers register for an account.
2. The seller reviews and approves buyer registration.
3. Approved buyers submit orders.
4. The seller checks stock and revises quantities or availability.
5. Buyers review and confirm the final order.
6. The seller generates an invoice.
7. After payment, the order is dispatched.

**Planned features:**
- Product catalog for multiple brands
- Power tools, hand tools, accessories, and spare parts
- Brand, category, subcategory, and price filters
- Stock and inventory management
- Buyer approval and account management
- Order revision and confirmation
- Invoice generation and order tracking
- GST-oriented billing, including applicable CGST and SGST
- Seller dashboard and buyer portal
- Responsive web experience

**Portfolio angle:** Complex commerce workflows, inventory synchronization, B2B ordering, and business process automation.

**Project status:** Product requirements and workflow design established; verify the implemented modules before displaying them as live features.
